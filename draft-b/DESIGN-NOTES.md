# Seven Summits Rwanda: World Class Redesign

## Handover status

This is a self-contained local design draft for review. It has not been deployed and the existing live website has not been changed.

Open `index.html` directly in a browser. The page uses one HTML file, local assets, inline CSS and inline JavaScript with no external dependencies.

## Design intent

The redesign presents Seven Summits as a premium Rwandan origin brand first and an online catalogue second. The visual system uses forest green, warm ivory and restrained brass with editorial serif type, generous spacing, detailed product imagery and quiet motion. The conversion path stays direct: understand the origin, choose a product and continue on WhatsApp.

## New patterns and why each exists

- Product-led cinematic hero: It establishes what Seven Summits sells within the first view while retaining the emotional power of the Rwandan landscape.
- Compact proof rail: It gives visitors immediate factual reassurance about origin, craft and physical availability without inventing social proof.
- Interactive tasting collection: It lets visitors compare the core tea and coffee range by character, format and price without sending them through separate pages.
- Editorial origin story: It turns altitude, hand picking and orthodox processing into a premium narrative that explains why the product deserves attention.
- Guided tea finder: It reduces choice anxiety with three simple questions and produces a relevant WhatsApp order path without claiming scientific personalisation.
- Corporate gifting studio: It transforms a vague enquiry into a structured brief covering gift type, quantity, timing, occasion and branding preference.
- Honest craft pairing: It connects the tea and gift objects to their making story without using an unfinished founder portrait or fictional biographies.
- Kigali boutique cards: They make the business feel physically verifiable and give local visitors clear places to shop.
- Order clarity accordion: It answers practical buying questions while openly asking customers to confirm any delivery and payment terms that were not supplied.
- Persistent mobile WhatsApp action: It keeps the primary sales channel within reach on small screens without covering the core content.
- Quiet reveal and product motion: It adds polish and depth while respecting reduced motion settings and keeping the interaction easy to control.

## Assumptions

- The product names, prices, descriptions, contact details and shop locations shown on the existing live page are current and approved.
- WhatsApp remains the primary direct sales route.
- The existing forest, ivory and brass palette is a recognisable part of the brand and should be elevated rather than replaced.
- The current product images may be reused in this redesign.
- The Rainforest Alliance mark already displayed on the current product range is approved for use, subject to final brand and certification confirmation.
- Product availability, delivery areas, fulfilment times and payment methods must be confirmed by the Seven Summits team before launch.
- The tea finder is a guided merchandising tool, not a diagnostic or guaranteed recommendation.

## Honest limits and needs backend

- WhatsApp is a handoff only and no order is stored by this page.
- Live stock, payment, delivery quoting and order tracking need backend services.
- Saved gifting briefs, customer accounts and automated follow-up need backend services.
- The location buttons open standard map searches and do not use a maps API key.
- No analytics, consent manager or advertising pixels are included in this local draft.

## Deliberately left out

- The unfinished founder portrait placeholder was removed because it reduced trust.
- Testimonials, reviews, awards, customer logos and performance claims were not invented.
- Shipping promises, returns terms, payment methods and delivery timelines were not invented.
- A custom domain and branded email were not presented because the supplied domain did not resolve during research and the current email is Gmail.
- Cinnamon was not foregrounded because it diluted the stronger single-origin Rwandan tea, coffee and gifting story.
- A full ecommerce checkout was not faked because stock, payment, delivery and order management require a backend.
- Founder, maker, estate and boutique portraits were not fabricated and should be commissioned before a final launch.
