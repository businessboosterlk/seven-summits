The Origin

Where altitude becomes flavour.

Our tea grows on the highest of those hills. Between 1,500 and 2,500 metres, cool air and misty mornings slow the leaf down. Slow growth concentrates flavour.

Every leaf is picked by hand. All of it, every harvest, as two leaves and a bud. The volcanic soil of the western province drives roots deep and gives the leaf its terroir, and everything we sell is single origin, made the orthodox way: whole leaf, never crushed.

Ordering, Answered

How ordering works.

How does an order begin?

Every Order button opens WhatsApp with your order already written out. You read it, you press send and we take it from there. Nothing is sent until you press send.

Where can I see the products in person?

At Mundi Centre on KG3 Avenue in Gikondo and at Izuba Boutique inside the Kigali Convention Centre.

Do you handle corporate and bulk gifts?

Yes. Tell us the quantity, the occasion and the deadline in the gifting form above and we reply with a quote, usually within the day.

How are delivery and payment handled?

Directly with us on WhatsApp when you order. Tell us where you are and we will confirm every detail with you before anything is paid.
